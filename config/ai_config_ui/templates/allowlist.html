Domain allow-list

Domains on the allow-list — and all their subdomains — are removed from the work queue (v_pending_observables) before n8n sees them: no VirusTotal request, no score, no e-mail. This is what keeps popular sites (google.com, wp.pl, ...) from using the VirusTotal free-tier quota (4 requests/min, 500/day).
  • Tranco entries — the top popular domains, refreshed every Saturday by the host cron job (playbook 04.4). Read-only here; the sync owns them.
  • Manual entries — your own additions (bank, employer, NAS vendor, ...). The weekly sync never touches them.
  • Exclusions — platforms where anyone can publish under a subdomain (github.io, pages.dev, ...). An exclusion beats every popular-site entry: evil.github.io is analysed even though github.io is a top site.
  • The most specific rule wins. A manual entry for one exact host overrides a broader exclusion: yt3.googleusercontent.com (manual) is skipped although googleusercontent.com is excluded. All other hosts on that platform are still analysed.
  • The whole mechanism can be switched off in Settings → Skip popular and trusted domains. Changes apply to the next workflow run.
